About Our Grievance Management System

Our Grievance Management System is a digital platform designed to make lodging and tracking complaints simple, transparent, and efficient.

What Can You Do?

📝 Lodge a Grievance

Users can submit their complaints by providing relevant details and uploading a photo of the issue.

🔍 Track Your Grievance

Users can track the progress of their complaint using a unique grievance ID.

📊 Transparent Status

The system can provide updates such as submitted, under review, assigned, in progress, and resolved.

Our Goal

The goal of this system is to provide an easy-to-use platform where complaints can be submitted, managed, monitored, and resolved efficiently.
